## Welcome!

I am a [Walter Benjamin](https://www.dfg.de/en/research-funding/funding-opportunities/programmes/individual/walter-benjamin) [research associate](https://www.sw.lmu.de/ifkw/de/das-institut/organisation-und-ansprechpartner/menschen-am-ifkw/kontaktseite/zhe-xu-b7d6b0a9.html) in the [Department of Media and Communication](https://www.ifkw.uni-muenchen.de/index.html) at [LMU Munich](https://www.lmu.de/en/), where I serve as PI of a [German Research Foundation (DFG)](https://www.dfg.de/) project. I am also a [research fellow](https://ptml.sjmc.wisc.edu/staff/xu-zhe/) at the [Public Tech Media Lab](https://ptml.sjmc.wisc.edu/), [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://www.wisc.edu/).

I hold degrees in engineering science and media studies and received my PhD in 2023 from the [University of Cologne](https://www.uni-koeln.de/en/), with joint supervision from the [University of East Anglia](https://www.uea.ac.uk/). At Cologne, I served as an associate lecturer in media studies and received training in natural language processing and machine learning from the [Center for Data and Simulation Science](https://cds.uni-koeln.de/en/) and the [Department of Digital Humanities](https://dh.phil-fak.uni-koeln.de/). I was also a visiting scholar at the [London School of Economics and Political Science (LSE)](https://www.lse.ac.uk/).

I work in political communication, computational social science, and computational journalism, with a substantive focus on information manipulation, platformization, and socially responsible technology, currently using multimodal text-as-data and natural language processing. My work has appeared in [Journalism & Mass Communication Quarterly](https://journals.sagepub.com/home/jmq) and [International Communication Gazette](https://journals.sagepub.com/home/gaz), among other peer-reviewed journals. The [DFG](https://www.dfg.de/), the [Alexander von Humboldt Foundation](https://www.humboldt-foundation.de/), and [LMU Munich](https://www.lmu.de/en/) have supported my work.
